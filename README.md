# Robotics Chassis Design

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **original CAD design and assembly of a modular robotic bot chassis** developed for a robotics application.

The complete chassis system was **independently designed and modelled from the ground up** according to the requirements of the intended robotic platform.

The design includes a **Main Chassis, Upper Deck, LiDAR Stand, Tablet/Screen Stand, and Front Bumper/Camera Mount**, with each component developed as part of the overall robotic platform.

During the design process, **weight optimization, structural requirements, component accessibility, and heat-transfer considerations** were taken into account to develop a practical and efficient chassis structure.

---

## 🧩 Designed Components

### 1. Main Chassis

The **Main Chassis** forms the primary structural foundation of the robotic platform.

It was designed to provide adequate support for the upper components while maintaining a lightweight structure. Cut-outs and openings were incorporated where appropriate to reduce unnecessary material and improve airflow around the internal components.

**Design considerations:**
- Structural support
- Weight reduction
- Component mounting
- Accessibility
- Airflow and heat dissipation

The **Upper Deck** provides an additional mounting platform above the main chassis.

### 3. LiDAR Stand

The **LiDAR Stand** is a dedicated elevated mounting structure designed to position the LiDAR sensor above the main body of the robot.

The stand was designed to provide sufficient support while avoiding unnecessary material, keeping the mounting structure lightweight.

**Design considerations:**
- LiDAR positioning
- Structural stability
- Weight optimization
- Clear sensor field of view

### 4. Tablet / Screen Stand

The **Tablet/Screen Stand** is a custom-designed support structure intended to hold a tablet or display screen on the robotic platform.

Its inclined geometry provides a suitable viewing angle while maintaining adequate structural support. The design also considers airflow around the mounted device and keeps the support structure relatively lightweight.

**Design considerations:**
- Screen support
- Viewing angle
- Structural stability
- Weight optimization
- Heat dissipation and airflow

The **Front Bumper** is designed to provide protection to the front portion of the robotic platform.

The geometry was developed while considering structural strength, weight, sensor positioning, and airflow around the front section.

## 🎯 Design Objectives

The main objectives of this design were:

- Develop a completely **original robotic chassis**
- Create a modular mechanical structure
- Optimize component weight without compromising the required structural support
- Provide dedicated mounting provisions for LiDAR, tablet/screen, and camera
- Consider **heat transfer and airflow** during component and structural design
- Maintain accessibility for assembly and maintenance
- Integrate multiple robotic components into a compact platform
- Verify the overall arrangement through CAD assembly modelling

---

## ⚙️ Key Design Considerations

### Weight Optimization

Weight was considered during the design of each component.

Material-saving cut-outs, optimized geometries, and suitable structural layouts were incorporated wherever possible to reduce unnecessary mass while maintaining the required support.

### Heat Transfer & Airflow

**Heat-transfer considerations** were also taken into account during the design process.

Openings, spacing, cut-outs, and component placement were considered to allow better airflow and reduce unnecessary obstruction around areas where electronic components and sensors may generate heat.

> Note: The design considerations described here are based on CAD-level mechanical design. No thermal simulation or experimental thermal validation is claimed unless separately documented in this repository.

### Component Integration

Each individual component was designed with the complete robotic system in mind.

Mounting locations, relative positioning, accessibility, and interaction between components were considered before creating the final assembly.
